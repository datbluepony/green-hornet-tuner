Questions

Straight answers.

Will a lope tune hurt how my truck starts or drives?

It shouldn't, if the cranking and cold-start tables stay stock and the in-gear idle keeps enough RPM and spark reserve. Stagline locks the start-up tables and the simulator scores stall margin when you drop into Drive with the A/C on. If a setting pushes the score down, you'll see it before anything is written.

Can I get lope from a stock cam?

Only a little. Lope comes from valve overlap. Idle tuning can make an existing cam sound more aggressive, but it can't create overlap that isn't there. The Lab shows this: switch to a stock cam in the Studio and the lope ceiling drops.

Does Stagline write to my ECU directly?

Not yet. In the preview you export an approved change sheet and apply it with a licensed flash tool (HP Tuners, EFILive), or use the mail-in service. Bluetooth OBD2 is read-only and can't change anything in the vehicle.

Which vehicles are supported?

2007–2013 GM trucks with Gen IV 4.8, 5.3, 6.0 and 6.2 engines on the E38 ECM are in preview, including LS swaps that use those ECMs. Gen III P01/P59 support is planned.

Who's responsible if something goes wrong?

You are. Stagline is experimental software and every write needs your approval. Advanced features and the mail-in service require a signed assumption-of-risk agreement. See the terms.
