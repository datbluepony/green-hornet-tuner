Removal

Pulling a Gen IV E38

  1. 1
    Key off, battery negative off.Wait 5 minutes. Never unplug an ECM with the battery connected.
  2. 2
    Find the ECM.On 2007–2013 GM trucks it's usually in the engine bay on the driver side, toward the front. In a swap (like a Wrangler), it's wherever the harness builder mounted it. Check your service manual.
  3. 3
    Unplug both connectors.Slide or lift each locking lever and pull straight out. Don't pry on the pins. Tape a plastic bag over each harness plug to keep it clean and dry.
  4. 4
    Unbolt the module.Remove the bracket bolts and keep the hardware in a labeled bag at home. Only the ECM gets shipped.
  5. 5
    Photo the label.Snap the sticker with the part and service numbers and attach it to your intake form.
Packing

What goes in the box

  1. ✓
    ECM in an anti-static bag.A clean zip bag is fine if that's all you have. No loose foam peanuts against the connectors.
  2. ✓
    Two inches of padding all around.Bubble wrap in a sturdy box. Ship nothing loose: no brackets, no harness.
  3. ✓
    Printed packing slip.Generated below. It has your order ID, return address and tune checksum.
  4. ✓
    Tracking + insurance.Insure for the ECM's replacement value (around $300–$500). Email us the tracking number.
  5. ✕
    Don't includeKeys, the vehicle's harness, payment or anything you need back besides the ECM.
The shipping address is sent with your order confirmation.
Getting it back

Reinstall & relearn

Bolt in & plug inConnectors fully seated, levers locked. Battery negative on last.
Key on, 30 secondsLet the module wake, then start. Don't touch the pedal.
Idle 3 minutesLets idle airflow relearn. A rough first minute is normal.
Crank, no start?Check battery and connectors first. GM trucks may need the anti-theft relearn: key ON 10 minutes, OFF 5 seconds, then start.
